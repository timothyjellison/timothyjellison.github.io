I've updated the color scheme here on my site from the generic whites and blues of the Minima theme to a more vibrant sand-in-your-eyes mix of **pink** and [green](#) against a dark background.

I've always enjoyed the clash of pink and green. It's got a 1980s vibe that I'm into. And not the synth-soaked Spandau Ballet 80s but the guitar hero 80s of Steve Vai and Joe Satriani back when they *both* had hair.

Check out this Ibanez JEM, one of Vai's signature guitars, and you'll see what I mean:

![Alt Text](https://i.pinimg.com/564x/67/33/49/673349c1b1941753001aa089cfe8808b.jpg)

**Pink** and [green](#). It defies good taste. It breaks rules. It rocks.
